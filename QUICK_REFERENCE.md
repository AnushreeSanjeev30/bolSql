# 📋 Quick Analytics Reference Card

## 🎯 13 Analytics You Can Use

### 1. **PROFIT ANALYSIS** 📊
**Ask:** "Subse zyada profit kaun sa item?"  
**Get:** Top items by profit, margin %, trends  
**Use:** Decide stock priorities

### 2. **FESTIVAL TRENDS** 🎉
**Ask:** "Diwali mein kya bikta hai?"  
**Get:** Sales during festival vs normal  
**Use:** Stock before festivals

### 3. **WEATHER IMPACT** 🌦️
**Ask:** "Baarish mein inventory kya?"  
**Get:** Seasonal demand patterns  
**Use:** Prepare for seasons

### 4. **DEAD STOCK** ⚠️
**Ask:** "Kaun sa maal nahi bik raha?"  
**Get:** Slow/unsold items, duration  
**Use:** Discount or remove items

### 5. **CUSTOMER PATTERNS** 👥
**Ask:** "Ghar kya regularly kharidta?"  
**Get:** Customer buying habits  
**Use:** Auto-order for regulars

### 6. **SMART REORDER** 📦
**Ask:** "Kitna order karna chahiye?"  
**Get:** Reorder qty, timing alerts  
**Use:** Never run out of stock

### 7. **SALES TREND** 📈
**Ask:** "Pichle 7 din sales kya?"  
**Get:** Daily revenue, peak day  
**Use:** Identify busy days

### 8. **RUSH HOURS** 🏪
**Ask:** "Kaunse baje sabse busy?"  
**Get:** Busiest hours breakdown  
**Use:** Schedule staff better

### 9. **TOP PRODUCTS** 🏆
**Ask:** "Sabse zyada kya bik?"  
**Get:** Top 10 sellers  
**Use:** Focus on bestsellers

### 10. **BUNDLE IDEAS** 🛒
**Ask:** "Log dono saath khareedta?"  
**Get:** Items bought together  
**Use:** Create combo deals

### 11. **CUSTOMER SEGMENTS** 💎
**Ask:** "Best customer kaun?"  
**Get:** VIP, loyal, at-risk customers  
**Use:** Reward loyalty programs

### 12. **CHURN RISK** 🚨
**Ask:** "Kaun nahi aa rahe bahut time?"  
**Get:** Customers likely to quit  
**Use:** Win them back offers

### 13. **STOCK DAYS LEFT** ⏰
**Ask:** "Kitne din ka stock bacha?"  
**Get:** Days remaining per item  
**Use:** Urgent reorder alerts
